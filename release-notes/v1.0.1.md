## Multron Download Manager v1.0.1

No CPU use while the window is idle or in the tray, less memory, IDM-style stream catching in the browser extension, dragging finished downloads out of the list, and a video download button that stays on the video.

### Performance

- **No CPU use while idle.** Looping effects such as the button shine, the title shimmer and the breathing selected row now pause when you are not using the window: when another window is in front, after 10 seconds without mouse or keyboard input, and while the window is closed to the tray or minimized. They continue the moment you move the mouse over the window. Before, they made WebView2 redraw the window 60 times a second even when nothing happened, which kept the fan running. In a test, an idle window went from about half a CPU core to 0%.
- **Looping effects are off by default.** Effects that keep moving as long as they are on screen are now off for new installs and are turned off once after the update: the moving background light, the light that follows the mouse, moving state colors, filling progress on cards, live indicators, the shine on progress bars and breathing accents. Effects that only play when something happens stay on: download card motion, opening animations, the click ripple, the glow under the mouse and the glass effect. No effect was removed; turn any of them back on in *Settings → Appearance → Animations*, where they look exactly as before and also pause when the window is idle.
- **Less memory in the tray.** When the window is closed to the tray or minimized, Multron Download Manager now tells WebView2 that it is not visible and asks it to use as little memory as possible. In a test, the window's WebView2 processes went from about 133 MB to about 92 MB in the tray. Opening the window brings everything back at once.
- **Lighter background images.** A background image is now resized to your screen and, if you blur it, blurred once when it is loaded instead of on every redraw. Large photos of your own no longer take up memory at their full size.

### Downloads

- **Drag finished downloads out of the list.** Drag a finished download to a folder, the desktop or another app (for example a chat or an email) to copy the file there, just like dragging it from File Explorer. With several downloads ticked, all of them go at once. The file stays where it is in Multron Download Manager. Files in quarantine or marked as a threat cannot be dragged out, just as they cannot be opened. Available on Windows.

### Browser extension

- **Catches video and audio streams from the page.** The extension now notices the video and audio a page actually loads, like IDM does: HLS (`.m3u8`) and DASH (`.mpd`) streams and media files such as MP4, WebM and MP3, with their size when the server tells it. They appear under *Caught on this page* in the download button's menu, so you can pick the exact stream. On sites the app does not know, where the player only plays a `blob:` address, the download button now uses the caught stream instead of trying to read the page again. Small files, ads and the little pieces a stream is made of are left out, YouTube keeps using its own better way, and the list is cleared when you go to another page.
- **Streams that need the page address work.** Caught streams are downloaded with the address of the page they came from and your browser's identification, as many video servers require. Other downloads are not changed.
- **The download button stays on the video.** The *Download* button over a video now sits inside the video player, so it scrolls, resizes and hides together with the video. Before, it could stay behind at an old spot on sites that scroll inside the page, such as YouTube, and cover menus, the search bar or the notifications list. Menus and pop-ups of the site now show above it.
